How Comparison Sites Make Money (It's Still Free For You)

You might wonder: If these services are free, how do they stay in business? The answer is commissions. When you purchase a policy through a comparison platform, the insurance carrier pays the platform a commission .

Important: These commissions come from the insurance companies' marketing budgets—not from your premiums. Your rates are the same as if you went directly to the carrier. There is never an upcharge to you .

Information You'll Need: For the most accurate quote, have ready: your driver's license number, vehicle VIN, current policy details (if any), and desired coverage levels. Anonymous quotes are only estimates and won't be as accurate .
