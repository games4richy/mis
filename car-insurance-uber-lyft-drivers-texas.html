The Three Periods of Rideshare Insurance Coverage

Understanding these three distinct coverage periods is crucial for Texas rideshare drivers. Each period activates different insurance policies with varying levels of protection .

Period 1

App On - Waiting for Request

Your Status: Logged into Uber/Lyft app but haven't accepted a ride

Rideshare Company Coverage: Limited liability coverage only ($50,000/$100,000/$25,000)

Personal Policy: Typically excludes commercial use during this period

The Gap: No coverage for your vehicle damage or comprehensive claims

Period 2

En Route to Pickup

Your Status: Accepted ride request, driving to passenger

Rideshare Company Coverage: $1 million liability coverage

Vehicle Damage: Contingent collision/comprehensive with $2,500 deductible

Requirement: You must carry personal collision/comprehensive coverage

Period 3

Passenger in Vehicle

Your Status: Passenger in car, traveling to destination

Rideshare Company Coverage: $1 million liability coverage

Vehicle Damage: Contingent collision/comprehensive with $2,500 deductible

Additional Protections: May include uninsured motorist coverage depending on state requirements

The Critical Coverage Gap in Period 1

Period 1 creates the most significant insurance risk for Texas rideshare drivers. While Uber and Lyft provide limited liability coverage during this phase, there's no coverage for your vehicle damage from collisions, theft, vandalism, or other comprehensive claims .

Most personal auto policies explicitly exclude commercial use, meaning you could be personally responsible for thousands of dollars in repair costs if an accident occurs while you're logged in but waiting for a ride request.

Texas-Specific Insurance Requirements

What Texas Law Requires for Rideshare Drivers

Texas transportation network company (TNC) regulations establish specific insurance requirements that Uber and Lyft must provide, but drivers also have personal responsibilities.

Personal Insurance Requirements

  • Must maintain personal auto insurance at Texas minimum liability limits: 30/60/25
  • Must provide proof of insurance to drive with Uber/Lyft
  • Recommended: Higher limits than state minimums for adequate protection

Rideshare Company Requirements in Texas

  • Period 1: $50,000 per person/$100,000 per accident bodily injury, $25,000 property damage
  • Periods 2 & 3: $1 million liability coverage
  • Vehicle Damage: Contingent comprehensive and collision with $2,500 deductible

What to Do After an Accident in Texas

Knowing the proper steps after an accident can protect your insurance claims and legal rights.

  • Ensure Safety First: Check for injuries and move to safe location if possible
  • Contact Emergency Services: Call police and paramedics if needed
  • Document Everything: Take photos of damage, accident location, and get other driver's information
  • Report Through App: Use Uber/Lyft safety toolkit to report crash immediately
  • Contact Your Insurer: Notify your personal insurance company about the accident
  • Seek Medical Attention: Even for minor injuries, get proper medical evaluation

Key Takeaways for Texas Rideshare Drivers

  • Standard personal auto policies exclude coverage during Period 1 (app on, no passenger), creating a significant insurance gap
  • Uber and Lyft provide $1 million liability coverage during trips but only limited coverage while waiting for requests
  • Rideshare endorsements cost 10-20% more than standard policies but prevent financial catastrophe from coverage gaps
  • Texas law requires specific coverage levels from both drivers and transportation network companies
  • Progressive, Mercury, State Farm, and Allstate offer Texas-specific rideshare endorsements with different benefits
  • Deductible gap coverage can save you $2,000 or more by paying the difference between your deductible and Uber/Lyft's $2,500 deductible

Proper insurance protection is not just a legal requirement for Texas rideshare drivers—it's essential financial security. By understanding the three coverage periods, choosing the right rideshare endorsement, and maintaining adequate personal coverage, you can drive with confidence knowing you're protected against the unique risks of transportation network company driving.
