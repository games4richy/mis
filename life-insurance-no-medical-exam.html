What Is No-Exam Life Insurance?

No-exam life insurance is exactly what it sounds like: a life insurance policy that doesn't require a medical exam. Instead of scheduling a nurse visit for blood draws and vitals, carriers use accelerated underwriting—AI algorithms that analyze your digital health data in real time .

When you apply, the carrier instantly checks:

  • Prescription drug databases — Your medication history for the past 7-10 years
  • MIB Group reports — Previous life insurance applications and findings
  • Motor vehicle records — Driving violations indicating risk behavior
  • Credit-based health indicators — Patterns correlating with health outcomes

The entire process happens in the background while you complete your application online. Most healthy applicants receive a decision in 5 to 15 minutes, and many carriers offer same-day coverage .

Types of No-Exam Life Insurance

Accelerated Underwriting (Best Option)

This is the gold standard for no-exam coverage. You complete a health questionnaire, and the carrier verifies your answers against third-party data sources automatically . If approved, you receive a fully underwritten policy at standard market rates .

  • Coverage limits: $100,000 to $3+ million
  • Best for: Healthy applicants under 60
  • Key advantage: Same rates as traditional policies

Simplified Issue

These policies use a short health questionnaire without pulling extensive medical records. Approval is faster, but premiums are higher and coverage limits lower .

  • Coverage limits: $5,000 to $500,000
  • Best for: Those with minor health conditions
  • Key advantage: Even faster approval than accelerated underwriting

Guaranteed Issue (Final Expense)

These policies accept everyone regardless of health—no health questions at all. They're designed for seniors or those with serious health conditions .

  • Coverage limits: $2,000 to $25,000
  • Best for: Ages 45-85 with health conditions
  • Key consideration: 2-year graded benefit period—full death benefit only after 2 years
  • Cost example: $15,000 policy for 65-year-old averages $90/month (women) to $116/month (men)

Who Qualifies for No-Exam Life Insurance?

Most healthy applicants under age 60 qualify for accelerated underwriting. Carriers typically approve no-exam applications for :

  • Ages: 18-60 (some carriers extend to 65 for smaller amounts)
  • Health conditions accepted: Controlled high blood pressure, well-managed type 2 diabetes, mild asthma, stable anxiety treated with medication, slightly elevated BMI

Common disqualifiers for no-exam approval include :

  • Recent cancer diagnosis or active treatment
  • Uncontrolled diabetes
  • Heart attack or stroke within the last few years
  • Multiple DUIs or serious driving violations
  • Alcohol or drug abuse/treatment
  • Bipolar disorder or other serious mental health conditions

Important: If you don't qualify for no-exam approval, you can still get coverage through traditional underwriting with a medical exam. Many carriers offer both paths .

Frequently Asked Questions

Is no-exam life insurance more expensive than traditional?
Not necessarily. For healthy applicants, accelerated underwriting often offers rates nearly identical to traditional policies. The difference is typically minimal—often just a few dollars per month .

How much coverage can I get without a medical exam?
Healthy applicants can get $1-3 million through accelerated underwriting. Banner Life offers up to $4 million, and Penn Mutual offers up to $10 million for qualified applicants .

How long does approval take?
Most applicants receive a decision in 5-15 minutes. Same-day coverage is available from many providers including Ethos, USAA, and Equitable .

Can I get life insurance if I have a pre-existing condition?
Yes. Many conditions like controlled high blood pressure, well-managed diabetes, and mild asthma are still eligible for no-exam coverage. For more serious conditions, guaranteed issue policies are available with no health questions .

What's the difference between accelerated underwriting and simplified issue?
Accelerated underwriting uses full medical underwriting without the exam—carriers pull your health data digitally. Simplified issue uses only health questions. Accelerated typically offers higher coverage limits and better rates .
