What Information You'll Need

According to InsuredBetter, having this information ready before you start can help the process move smoothly and efficiently :

  • Your date of birth, gender, and marital status
  • The make, model, and year of your vehicle(s)
  • Vehicle identification numbers (VINs)
  • The expected annual mileage for each vehicle
  • How the vehicle is used (commuting, leisure, etc.)
  • The date of birth for all drivers who will use the insured vehicle
  • Your address and where the vehicle is stored
  • Accidents, tickets, and claims histories of all drivers
  • Driver's license numbers for all drivers
  • Your current car insurance company and coverage amounts

Important: "Car insurance quotes are only as good as the information you provide. Not providing enough information or providing inaccurate details can lead to being misquoted" . Always be honest and accurate when requesting quotes.

How Comparison Sites Make Money

You might wonder how these services stay in business without charging you. The answer is commissions . When you purchase an insurance policy through a comparison platform, the insurance carrier pays the platform a commission—this comes from their marketing budget, not an upcharge to you. Your rates are the same as if you went directly to the carrier .

When to Get a Free Insurance Quote

Industry experts recommend getting quotes :

  • At every renewal — Usually every 6-12 months
  • After major life changes — Moving, getting married, adding a teen driver
  • When your credit score improves — Can significantly lower premiums
  • After a ticket falls off your record — Usually after 3-5 years
  • When you pay off your car loan — You may choose to drop collision/comprehensive

"It's recommended to compare car insurance quotes every six to 12 months, or before your policy renews, to ensure you're still getting the best rate" .
