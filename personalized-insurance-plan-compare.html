How Personalized Insurance Comparison Works

Traditional insurance shopping required you to visit multiple websites and re-enter the same information over and over. New AI-powered platforms flip this model .

How Comparison Sites Make Money (It's Still Free For You)

You might wonder how these services stay in business without charging you. The answer is commissions . When you purchase a policy through a comparison platform, the insurance carrier pays the platform a commission—this comes from their marketing budget, not an upcharge to you. Your rates are the same as if you went directly to the carrier.
